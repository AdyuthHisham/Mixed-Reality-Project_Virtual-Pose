## Limb Tracking
1. The Enjalbert test consists of five main exercises:
	* Raise the affected arm to a specific height and hold it in a static position
	*  Bring the affected hand to the mouth
	*  Open and close the affected hand
	*  Touch the index finger and middle finger with the thumb
	*  Touch the ring finger and little finger with the thumb
	- *All of these exercises are designed to test upper body mobility along with hand mobility and grip strength.* **(Enjalbert et al. 1988)**
